Summary
Sorting algorithms are fundamental techniques used to arrange data in a specific order, such as ascending or descending.
They improve the efficiency of searching, data processing, and analysis. Different sorting algorithms have different 
strengths depending on the size and nature of the data. Simple algorithms like Bubble Sort, Selection Sort, and Insertion
Sort are easy to understand but are less efficient for large datasets. Advanced algorithms such as Merge Sort, Quick Sort, 
and Heap Sort provide much better performance for handling larger amounts of data. Choosing the appropriate sorting algorithm
depends on factors such as time complexity, space complexity, stability, and the size of the input.

Conclusion
Sorting algorithms are an essential part of computer science and software development. Understanding their working
principles and complexities helps in selecting the most suitable algorithm for a particular application. While simple
sorting algorithms are useful for learning and small datasets, advanced algorithms like Merge Sort, Quick Sort, and
Heap Sort are preferred in real-world applications due to their better efficiency. Overall, studying sorting algorithms
enhances problem-solving skills and builds a strong foundation in data structures and algorithm design.
